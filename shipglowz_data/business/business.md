# TemuGlowz Business Context

## Audience and intent

TemuGlowz targets shoppers looking for budget-friendly Temu discoveries, themed product selections, gift ideas, and practical accessories. Search intent is primarily commercial discovery around gadgets, kitchen accessories, seasonal products, and shopping organization.

## Positioning

TemuGlowz is an independent selection and comparison project. It does not claim Temu approval, partnership, or authorization before written confirmation. Product selections are editorial curation, not hands-on testing unless evidence is linked.

## Business model

Affiliate monetization is considered only after program approval, with clear disclosure near qualifying recommendations and no unsupported commission or partnership claim.

## Claim boundary

Price, availability, seller status, delivery, suitability, and performance are volatile or unverified unless the editorial claim register records current evidence.

Update this artifact when audience, positioning, monetization, compliance posture, or public promise changes.
