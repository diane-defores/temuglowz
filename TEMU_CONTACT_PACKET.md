# Temu Contact Packet

## Goal

Get a clear written answer from Temu on what TemuGlowz is allowed to do legally and officially for product discovery, enrichment, and affiliate publishing.

## Context

TemuGlowz is building French editorial guide pages around Temu products.

The operational need is not just affiliate links. We need to know whether Temu offers an official way to access product data for:

- product discovery
- product metadata enrichment
- deep links
- images
- descriptions
- reviews or ratings
- ongoing product updates

## What We Need To Learn

Ask Temu for explicit confirmation on these points.

### 1. Affiliate Data Access

- Do you offer an official product feed for affiliates or publishers?
- Do you offer an affiliate API for product search, product details, or deep linking?
- Do you offer any catalogue export for approved affiliates or content publishers?

### 2. Partner Access

- Is the Temu Partner Platform available only to sellers and marketplace partners, or can editorial publishers also apply?
- If publishers can apply, what program or approval path should we use?
- Is there a separate API program for editorial comparison or discovery websites?

### 3. Allowed Content Usage

- Can we store and display product title, product URL, main image, gallery images, and description excerpts on our editorial pages?
- Can we store price snapshots with a visible date and a disclaimer that price and availability may change?
- Can we quote rating and review-count data if we include a date and source link?
- Can we use short review snippets, or is that prohibited without special approval?

### 4. Technical Access Rules

- Is automated product-page scraping prohibited even for approved affiliates?
- If scraping is prohibited, what official alternative do you recommend for publisher product enrichment?
- Are there approved deep-linking or product-lookup endpoints for known product URLs or product IDs?

### 5. Rights And Limits

- Are there restrictions on storing product images locally for performance optimization?
- Are there restrictions on using Temu product content in SEO landing pages or editorial buying guides?
- What disclosures are required for affiliate content that references Temu products?

## Best-Case Outcome

The ideal written response would confirm one of these:

1. Temu offers an official affiliate or publisher feed/API we can apply for.
2. Temu allows a specific limited editorial use of product metadata and images.
3. Temu provides a partner-contact path for a publisher integration request.

## Minimum Useful Outcome

Even a negative answer is useful if it clearly states:

- no affiliate feed exists
- no publisher API exists
- scraping is not allowed
- only manual linking is allowed

That would let us design the workflow correctly.

## Suggested Message In French

```md
Bonjour,

Je travaille sur un site éditorial en français qui publie des guides de sélection de produits Temu avec une logique de recommandation et, à terme, un usage affilié si cela est autorisé.

Avant d’aller plus loin, je voudrais vérifier quelles solutions officielles Temu propose aux éditeurs ou affiliés pour accéder légalement aux données produit.

Pouvez-vous me préciser les points suivants :

1. Proposez-vous un flux produit, une API catalogue, ou une API affilié pour les éditeurs / affiliés ?
2. La Partner Platform Temu est-elle réservée aux vendeurs, ou existe-t-il un accès possible pour un site éditorial / comparatif ?
3. Avons-nous le droit d’afficher sur nos pages éditoriales :
   - le nom du produit,
   - l’URL produit,
   - l’image principale ou plusieurs images,
   - une courte description,
   - une note moyenne et un nombre d’avis datés,
   - un relevé de prix indicatif avec mention de date ?
4. Le scraping automatisé des fiches produit est-il interdit même pour un affilié approuvé ?
5. Si oui, quelle est l’alternative officielle recommandée par Temu pour enrichir légalement des fiches éditoriales produit ?
6. Existe-t-il des restrictions spécifiques concernant :
   - la conservation locale des images produit,
   - l’usage SEO de contenus produit sur des guides éditoriaux,
   - la citation d’avis ou extraits d’avis,
   - les obligations de disclosure affilié ?

Si vous avez une documentation partenaire, un contact business, ou un programme adapté aux éditeurs de contenu, je suis preneuse.

Merci.
```

## Suggested Message In English

```md
Hello,

I am working on a French editorial website that publishes curated buying guides featuring Temu products, with possible affiliate usage if approved.

Before going further, I would like to confirm what official Temu options exist for publishers or affiliates to access product data legally.

Could you please clarify the following:

1. Do you offer a product feed, catalogue API, or affiliate API for publishers or affiliates?
2. Is the Temu Partner Platform limited to sellers, or is there a valid access path for editorial or comparison websites?
3. Are we allowed to display on editorial pages:
   - product name,
   - product URL,
   - primary image or multiple images,
   - a short product description,
   - dated rating and review-count data,
   - a dated price snapshot with a clear disclaimer?
4. Is automated scraping of product pages prohibited even for approved affiliates?
5. If yes, what official alternative does Temu recommend for legal product enrichment on editorial pages?
6. Are there specific restrictions regarding:
   - local storage of product images for performance optimization,
   - SEO usage of product content in editorial buying guides,
   - quoting review snippets,
   - affiliate disclosure requirements?

If there is a relevant partner document, business contact, or publisher-facing program, I would be glad to review it.

Thank you.
```

## Where To Send

Try these in this order:

1. affiliate program support or contact form
2. partner platform contact path
3. business or publisher contact if Temu exposes one

## After Reply

When Temu answers, record:

- the date
- the exact contact channel
- the exact wording of permissions or refusals
- any links to official docs
- whether the answer applies to affiliates, publishers, sellers, or researchers

## Decision Rule

Do not assume that affiliate approval means scraping permission.

Do not assume that partner API visibility means TemuGlowz is eligible to use it.

Only treat a data-access path as approved if Temu confirms it explicitly in writing.
